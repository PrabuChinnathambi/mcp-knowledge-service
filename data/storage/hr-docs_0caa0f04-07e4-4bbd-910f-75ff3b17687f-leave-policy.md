# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

## Carry Forward
A maximum of 12 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year.
Carried-forward days expire on 31 March of the following year.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
